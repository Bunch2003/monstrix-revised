## What changed from the original firmware
Rewritten animation engine and 40+ animations, screen effects, web remote with
live screen mirror, sunset-based day/night and night dimming, PlayStation Network
presence and trophy reactions, wireless (OTA) updates, Wi-Fi auto-reconnect, and
support for two robots (Monstrix and Nova) from one sketch.
